# Settings appearance implementation inventory

This inventory maps the Settings widget routes to the material they should use. It is a source-level checklist, not a claim of device screenshot verification. The appearance selector determines the material: `GlassChrome.enabled(app)` for Frosted Glass, with the existing theme tokens for all other looks. `GlassSurface` belongs around large independent groups; nested rows, fields and menus reuse `GlassChrome` fills and edges to avoid stacking blur filters. High contrast and TV fallbacks remain owned by `GlassSurface`.

## Cross-screen ownership

- Home and Search large feedback and action controls: `search_screen.dart` / `_HomeGlassState` and `_HomeGlassAction` use the same fill and edge when Frosted Glass is selected.
- Discover and Library cards, Downloads poster grid and menus: reuse `see_all` cards, `GlassChrome`-aware shared filters and Material theme; keep backdrop decoding and cache lifetimes independent of chrome.
- Reels and detail overlays: reserve direct glass/blur for interactive overlays and use stable scrims over video; playback lifecycle and video texture stay independent of appearance.
- Sharing and navigation are functional contracts: IMDb title links through `ReelShareService`, Settings excluded from tab paging, and route depth overriding shell horizontal gestures.

## Validation boundary

Source-level inventory and widget tests can verify theme branching, colors, menu ownership and route behavior. Full contrast, blur, video first frames, native platform views, DPAD hardware focus and per-device performance still require real-device QA. Existing golden baselines for Spotlight are a separate look (`SeparationModel.fill`); do not regenerate them merely because Frosted Glass changed.
